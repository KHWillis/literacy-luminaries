# The Literacy Luminaries — Project Brief

## Source of truth
See `prelim.txt` for the full vision doc from the founders. This brief distills it
into something we can plan against, and should be updated as decisions are made.

## Who's involved
- **Founders (client):** Three literacy professionals, including your mother, launching a
  retirement/side business.
- **You (technical lead):** Handling domain, hosting, and building the site.
  C#/.NET and Python background, some front-end experience, wants a stack that is
  low-maintenance long-term (few servers to patch, minimal on-call surface).

## Business summary
- **Name:** The Literacy Luminaries (name/domain may still change)
- **Tagline:** Lighting the Way to Literacy
- **Positioning:** Literacy experts & academic therapists — explicitly *not* a tutoring service.
- **Three service pillars** (don't all need to launch at once):
  1. **Academic Therapy** — individualized literacy intervention (reading, dyslexia,
     spelling, handwriting, written language). No math.
  2. **Literacy Resources & Curriculum** — future original materials for sale, including a
     flagship dysgraphia/writing intervention framework.
  3. **Professional Learning** — future training/consultation/courses for educators, parents,
     and possibly schools/districts.
- **Brand voice:** Professional, sophisticated, warm, knowledgeable, approachable, credible,
  modern. Explicitly *not* a juvenile/tutoring-center look (no primary colors, ABC blocks,
  crayons, cartoon lightbulbs).
- **Palette:** Burgundy (dominant) • Pink (accent) • Black • Gray • White
- **Visual motifs:** light/illumination/rays/pathways/growth/books, subtly built around
  the number three (three founders). Nothing literal or cheesy.
- **Important messaging guardrails:**
  - Never imply "your child just needs more practice."
  - Emphasize: identify need → explicit instruction → monitor response → adjust → build
    independence/confidence.
  - Distinguish Academic Therapy from tutoring without disparaging tutors.
  - Don't over-anchor the brand on "dyslexia" — literacy is the umbrella (reading, spelling,
    dysgraphia/handwriting, written expression, structured literacy, PD, and future offerings).

## V1 site structure (launch)
`Home | About Us | Academic Therapy | Resources | Professional Learning | Contact`

## Known future expansion (don't design ourselves into a corner)
- Nav: Shop | Courses/Training | Our Writing Program | Parent Resources | Educator Resources
- Features: online scheduling/intake, payments, digital resource sales, curriculum sales,
  educator course delivery, PD registration, email/newsletter sign-up.

## Homepage must communicate (within seconds)
1. Who they are + tagline
2. That the work is specialized, evidence-based, and individualized
3. The three pathways (Academic Therapy / Professional Learning / Literacy Resources)

## "Meet the Luminaries" page
Each founder: professional photo, short bio, credentials, a personal touch. Goal: "These
women know literacy" + "I would trust them with my child."

## Guiding constraint
V1 should be beautiful, professional, simple, and **easy to maintain** — both for you
(technical maintenance) and for the founders (content updates like bios, blog posts, maybe
resources later) without needing you for every small change.
